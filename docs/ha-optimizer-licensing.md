# Home Assistant optimiser access

PowerSync Home Assistant optimiser access is separate from PowerSync Cloud.
The subscription covers one active Home Assistant installation; multiple
PowerSync battery entries on that installation share access. Cloud subscriptions do not
grant HA optimiser access.

## Subscribe and pair Home Assistant

Choose either a direct A$4/month subscription or a qualifying recurring GitHub
sponsorship of at least US$3/month. Direct signup requires a card and offers one
seven-day trial, then bills automatically until cancelled. GitHub sponsorship
is charged immediately and has no HA trial. See the
[setup and subscription guide](https://powersync.cc/ha-optimiser).

In the integration options, open **HA optimiser access** and select **Start
pairing**. Keep this dialog open. Approve the code on your [PowerSync account
page](https://powersync.cc/app/ha), then select **Finish pairing** in the
original HA dialog within ten minutes. If the dialog has closed or the code has
expired, start again with a new code.

If you previously disconnected **this HA server** on your account page and want
to reconnect it, select **Reset pairing** in HA, then **Start pairing**. Approve
the new code and finish pairing in the same HA dialog. If another installation
is listed and you cannot identify it, contact support. To check access for an
already paired server, select **Refresh access**.

## Expiry and recovery

Monitoring, dashboards, manual controls and recovery remain available without
an optimiser subscription. Existing verified access can continue through a
bounded offline period; the status shown in Home Assistant determines when
access expires.

When access ends, new optimiser work stops and PowerSync attempts to restore
only the temporary settings and sessions it owns. Recovery can remain pending
when hardware is unavailable. Restoring paid access does not automatically
restart an old schedule: complete recovery and select **Resume optimisation**
when requested. Smart Optimization and Monitoring Mode settings still apply.

Manage cancellation through the billing portal or GitHub, according to the
payment method you chose. Paid subscriptions and qualifying sponsors include
priority HA support, without a guaranteed response time.

## Refer friends

Open **HA optimiser access** in the integration options to see your account's
referral code and link. Administrators can also use the **Refer friends** card
on the PowerSync dashboard. The same code is available in **Referrals** on your
PowerSync account, including from Cloud settings.

Your friend can choose Home Assistant or Cloud and must add the code before
their first subscription payment. Only you earn a free month, after that payment
succeeds. Trial periods and free invoices do not earn rewards. If you already
subscribe through Stripe, your next full monthly renewal is free automatically.
If you subscribe to both products when your friend pays, both receive a free
renewal, including the current Cloud plan.

Free accounts can earn and bank rewards. Activate a banked reward for one
product from the referral page by verifying a card and agreeing to automatic
renewal. No initial payment is required; normal monthly billing starts after
the free month unless you cancel. GitHub sponsorship alone does not redeem
Stripe rewards.

You can earn up to 12 rewards each UTC calendar year across both products.
Unused rewards do not expire and cover successive renewals. Self-referrals,
detected shared payment identities and repeated paying accounts do not qualify.
A refunded or disputed referral payment can reverse unused rewards. New trials
require card verification and are limited per account and detected payment
identity for each product. Referral information being unavailable does not
change your existing optimiser access.

Minimal hashed account and payment-identity receipts are retained after account
deletion to enforce trial limits and first-payment eligibility. Full card details
stay with Stripe. Recovering earned rewards after deletion requires verification
of your PowerSync sign-in email. If separate legacy accounts share an identity,
contact PowerSync support to link them before claiming rewards.
