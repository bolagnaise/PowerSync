Tesla BLE bridge installer

Install the PowerSync Tesla Bluetooth bridge firmware on an ESP32 straight from this page. No ESPHome dashboard, YAML or passwords needed.

You need
  • An ESP32, ESP32-S3, ESP32-C5 or ESP32-C6 board, for example an M5Stack Atom S3 or NanoC6.
  • A USB data cable (some cables only charge).
  • Chrome or Edge on a computer.
Your browser can't install firmware. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer. This page must be opened over HTTPS.

After the install, enter your Wi-Fi when the installer asks.

1. Add it to Home Assistant

Home Assistant finds the new Tesla BLE device under Settings > Devices & services. Select Configure to add it. The device is named Tesla BLE plus the last six characters of its address, for example Tesla BLE dd95d4. PowerSync finds a single bridge by itself. With two or more bridges, enter each one's entity prefix (for example tesla_ble_dd95d4) in PowerSync's Tesla BLE settings.

2. Set your car

If PowerSync already knows your Tesla (through Tesla Fleet, Teslemetry or Tessie), it fills in the bridge's Tesla VIN setting within about 15 minutes. Otherwise, open the Tesla BLE device and type your 17-character VIN into Tesla VIN. The bridge then finds your car over Bluetooth by itself.

3. Pair it with your car

Sit in the car with your key card. On the Tesla BLE device in Home Assistant, press Pair BLE key, then tap the key card on the centre console and accept the new key on the car's screen.

Replacing an existing bridge? If you still have its ESPHome YAML, update it wirelessly instead: see Update your Tesla BLE bridge. Installing from this page erases the board, so you pair the car again (step 3).

The bridge's charging-current maximum is 48 A. Your car still applies its own charger limit, so it never charges faster than your charger allows.
