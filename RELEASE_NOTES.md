## Clearer average energy cost

The dashboard now labels the Today so far average as “Average net energy cost” and explains that it is the net cost per kWh used at home. This avoids confusing the household cost, which can include a daily supply charge, with the price paid to charge the battery.

Update through HACS and restart Home Assistant. This wording correction does not change battery cost tracking or export decisions.
