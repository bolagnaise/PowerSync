## Save battery energy for expensive household use

Smart Optimization could charge during cheap hours and then use that energy for the house before the higher-price period it was meant to cover. PowerSync now preserves the planned hold when the battery is scheduled to supply the house later at a higher import price, including peaks more than six hours away.

Update through HACS and restart Home Assistant. This correction applies when Idle is allowed; Disable Idle continues to permit natural self-consumption.
