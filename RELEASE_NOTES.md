## GloBird current export value

The Right now grid tile now includes your configured Super Export rate while the bonus window is active and measured allowance remains. It returns to the base feed-in rate when the allowance is exhausted or the window ends; solar and battery export share that allowance.

Incomplete or stale allowance history is labelled **bonus unknown**, rather than promising a premium rate. Update through HACS, restart Home Assistant and refresh the dashboard.
