## One Support card

Temporary support sessions, ongoing diagnostics and local diagnostic recording now share one Support card. Select **Allow ongoing support** to set up ongoing read-only access instead of using a separate HA diagnostics card.

Existing approvals and revoke controls remain available in either mode. Selecting the option does not grant or revoke access; you still review and approve the requested permissions in Home Assistant. Saved HA diagnostics cards also open the unified interface.

Update through HACS and restart Home Assistant, then refresh the dashboard.
