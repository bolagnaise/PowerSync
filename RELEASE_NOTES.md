## What's Changed

**Seasonal electricity pricing**
AGL and Custom TOU users can now create named calendar seasons with different time periods, import rates, and feed-in rates. Year-wrapping schedules such as AGL High Season from November through March are supported, while existing All Year tariffs continue unchanged.

**Season-aware mobile tariff editing**
PowerSync now accepts seasonal tariff updates from PowerSync Mobile, validates that every month is covered without overlaps, and safely reapplies AGL Battery Rewards export pricing across every season.

Update available via HACS
