## Refer friends from Home Assistant

Share your referral code from HA optimiser access or the Refer friends dashboard card after pairing your PowerSync account. A friend can sign up for HA or Cloud; their first eligible payment earns you a free month. Only the referrer receives a reward.

Stripe subscribers receive a free full monthly renewal. If you subscribe to both products when your friend pays, both receive a free renewal. Free accounts can bank rewards and activate one chosen product with a verified card and automatic renewal, without an initial payment. The account referral page shows your rewards and the shared limit of 12 rewards per calendar year.

## Trials and availability

New seven-day trials require card verification. Existing trial deadlines stay the same. Trials, self-referrals and detected shared payment identities do not earn rewards.

Update through HACS, restart Home Assistant and refresh the dashboard to load the new card. Referral information being unavailable does not change your existing optimiser access.
