## Clearer battery command labels

Charging and discharging started by the optimiser could appear as “Manual override” and “on your command”. Right now and Battery controls now identify commands from the optimiser, manual controls, PowerSync automations and price spike control, with neutral wording when the source is unknown.

Starting and restoring messages remain visible. The countdown describes the current command, which can change when the optimiser replans. Update through HACS, restart Home Assistant and refresh the dashboard.
