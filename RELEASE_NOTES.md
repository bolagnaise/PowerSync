## Sigenergy EV charging and battery export

Price-Level charging now releases its home-battery preserve request after a fresh confirmed Sigenergy EVAC disconnection. This prevents an old charging session from continuing to block optimizer export after the car is unplugged.

Pending charger commands, uncertain readings, and another charging mode's valid preserve request keep the battery hold in place. Update through HACS and restart Home Assistant.
