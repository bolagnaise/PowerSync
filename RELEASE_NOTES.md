## What's Changed

**Align Smart Schedule previews with executable policy**
The EV schedule preview now uses the saved Smart Schedule grid-price ceiling, so displayed grid windows and feasibility match the plan regenerated by the executor. Runtime evaluation also keeps the departure-day strategy and price policy across midnight, while preserving existing ownership, demand, battery, and monitoring gates.

**Restore all Android day-override choices**
The Smart Schedule day and strategy menus now use a scrollable action list instead of Android's three-button alert limit. Existing choices—including Cheapest, Meet Deadline, Use Default, constraints, and Clear Day—remain reachable without changing the strategy economics or adding a new price-ceiling setting.
