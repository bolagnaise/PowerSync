## Solar recharge in evening export plans

Daytime plans made while the battery is low now account for solar energy stored before protected evening export periods. This lets the optimizer use better evening feed-in prices while keeping energy reserved for later household use and preserving grid-charge/export safeguards.

Update through HACS and restart Home Assistant. Plans continue to adjust as battery levels, prices and forecasts change.
