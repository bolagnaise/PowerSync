## What's Changed

**Leave away vehicles charging under local control**
PowerSync Price-Level Charging now treats an away vehicle as outside its control scope instead of as a request to stop. Charging at work, a relative's home, or another non-home location is left untouched whether the session was externally started or still appears in PowerSync's local state. Existing at-home high-price stopping remains unchanged.
