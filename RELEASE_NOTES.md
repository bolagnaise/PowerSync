## Administrator access for support repairs

The Home Assistant owner can now approve an investigator to diagnose and repair the installation using temporary administrator access in the PowerSync Support card. Access renews while the investigator stays connected. The owner can stop it at any time, and the investigator can log out when the repair is done.

Repairs require a Home Assistant HTTPS address the investigator can reach. Update through HACS and restart Home Assistant.
