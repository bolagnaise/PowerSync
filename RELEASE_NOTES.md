## Tesla Smart Schedule charge targets

While Smart Schedule is on, changing the charge target in PowerSync updates the paired Tesla, and changing the charge limit in Tesla updates the schedule. If the two limits differ when first linked, PowerSync uses the lower one. Turning Smart Schedule off stops synchronization.

A sleeping or disconnected Tesla catches up when it next reports. Update PowerSync through HACS and restart Home Assistant.
