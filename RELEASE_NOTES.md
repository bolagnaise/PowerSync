## What's Changed

**Optional live solar forecast correction**
Live solar forecast correction is now off by default, including after updating. To keep the previous behavior, enable **Live solar forecast correction** under **Configure > Advanced settings > Weather & solar forecast**. When enabled, the correction reduces the planning forecast after low live production, tapers across the next six hours and can bring grid charging forward.

Reserve limits, Charge By Time safety allowances and curtailment rules continue to apply. Update through HACS and restart Home Assistant.
