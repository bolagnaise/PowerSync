## What's Changed

**AGL tariffs now refresh in the Sigenergy app after setup changes**
When a Sigenergy system switches to AGL Battery Rewards, PowerSync now uploads
the saved AGL schedule once after setup or reload instead of only updating its
local optimiser prices. The upload respects automatic-sync and monitoring-mode
controls, while retaining the existing manual tariff-sync service.

Update available via HACS
