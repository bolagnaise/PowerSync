## What's Changed

**Smart Optimization settings**
Enabling Smart Optimization from the mobile app now shows an access error immediately if HA optimiser access is unavailable or a Resume is required, instead of appearing to turn on and then reverting. Open **HA optimiser access** in the PowerSync integration options to check pairing and access, complete any pending recovery, and resume optimisation when required. This update does not change access eligibility or expiry.
