## Today’s weather in Right now

The dashboard now shows today’s temperature and forecast high/low. Open the weather chip for hourly cloud cover and today’s dawn, sunrise, sunset and dusk in your Home Assistant timezone. Missing forecast hours and outdated values are labelled; daylight remains available without a weather entity.

## Accurate live power and car labels

Self-consumption now describes solar powering the home and charging the battery when live readings show those flows. Energy-flow backgrounds without a car hide its readings instead of placing them over the house.

Update through HACS and restart Home Assistant. The weather chip is also supported by iOS and Android app build 469 or later.
