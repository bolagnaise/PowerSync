## Solar forecasts

Live solar forecast correction is now off by default, including after updating.
PowerSync no longer reduces the forecast because of a temporary dip in live
production unless you enable **Live solar forecast correction** under
**Weather & solar forecast**. When enabled, the correction can bring grid charging
forward and tapers across the next six hours of the forecast. Existing reserve,
Charge By Time and curtailment safeguards continue to apply.
