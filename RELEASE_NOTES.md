## Tesla Bluetooth recovery on Home Assistant 2026.11.3

After updating Home Assistant to 2026.11.3, a Tesla could stay stuck on “Waiting for transport recovery” after a Bluetooth command went unconfirmed. PowerSync can now restart a supported bridge and verify fresh vehicle readings on this version so that automatic charging can resume.

Update through HACS and restart Home Assistant.
