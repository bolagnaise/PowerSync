## Longer Force Discharge windows

Force Discharge now offers presets through six hours. Choose Custom and set Force Discharge Custom Duration, or enter a whole-minute duration directly in the service, up to 24 hours where your device supports it. Unsupported durations return a clear error. Amber overrides retain their four-hour limit, and other native device limits still apply.

Charge keeps its existing choices. Hold and Self Consumption now have a separate duration picker, initially set to your previous shared selection.

## Full-day Tesla discharge

Fixed a full-day discharge tariff wrapping into a short window when started between half-hour boundaries. The requested countdown and existing reserve, cancellation and restore safeguards still apply.
