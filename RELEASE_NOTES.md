## Planned battery power chart shows the full 48 hours again

The Planned battery power chart was only showing about the next 16 to 18 hours, even though it is labelled "Next 48 hours". Plans are now made in 5-minute steps, and the chart was cutting them off after a fixed number of bars. It now always shows the full 48 hours, with each bar covering 15 minutes so the chart stays readable.

Hover, tap, or use the arrow keys to see the time and power for any bar. Update through HACS, restart Home Assistant, and refresh the dashboard.
