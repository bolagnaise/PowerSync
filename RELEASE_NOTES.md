## Clearer planned power on the dashboard

Charge and export windows now show their lowest and highest planned power when the rate varies between slots. A window containing 100 W and 5 kW targets shows **100 W–5.0 kW**, so it no longer appears to promise a steady 5 kW throughout.

This corrects the display; it does not change the optimiser's targets or battery controls. Update through HACS, restart Home Assistant and refresh the dashboard.
