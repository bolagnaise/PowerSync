## Graph tooltips restored on the new dashboard

The redesigned Home Assistant dashboard now shows time and value tooltips on **Prices today**, **Energy since midnight**, **Load forecast**, **Planned battery power**, and **Solar and load forecast**. These graphs were missing their tooltip interactions after the redesign.

Hover over or tap a graph to read its values, or focus it and use the left and right arrow keys. Press Escape to dismiss the tooltip. Tooltips stay within the graph edges, use the correct price or power units, and show charging or discharging for battery bars.

Update through HACS and restart Home Assistant. Hard-refresh your browser if the tooltips do not appear.
