## Readable battery plan labels near midnight

The Planned battery power chart now keeps time labels from overlapping when a plan starts close to midnight. Labels also stay inside the chart on narrow cards and adapt to 12-hour or 24-hour time formats.

Hover, tap, or use the arrow keys to see the exact time for any bar. Update through HACS, restart Home Assistant, and refresh the dashboard.
