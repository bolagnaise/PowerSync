## PowerSync notification settings

The PowerSync mobile app can now choose which PowerSync push notifications to receive from this Home Assistant site. Each phone saves its own choices.

Home Assistant administrators can also turn off the PowerSync update available notice for the site. The update entity and scheduled updates remain available.

## Battery and grid export on the dashboard

The Home Assistant dashboard now shows when the battery is discharging and the site is exporting at the same time.

Update through HACS and restart Home Assistant.
