## Clearer Support setup

The Support card now guides you through pasting an invitation, sending a pairing code, and reviewing the investigator before approving read-only access. The invitation field is easier to find, the pairing code is large and copyable, and the card refreshes automatically while a session is pending or active.

Investigator details and sharing choices are shown in plain language. Local recording and diagnostic activity sit in separate expandable sections, and the card explains that 30 minutes is the setup deadline, not a waiting period. You still choose what to share and explicitly approve access in Home Assistant.

Update through HACS and restart Home Assistant.
