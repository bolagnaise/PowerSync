## Power limit slider

Battery controls now use a slider for the manual power limit, making it quicker to move from Max to a value such as 10 kW. The kW readout follows the slider, and the selection is saved when you release it. Set it to 0 for Max.

The slider uses your system's supported range and increments. Update through HACS, restart Home Assistant and refresh the dashboard.
