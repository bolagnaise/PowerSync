## Tariff chart alignment

The Tariff today colour strip now lines up with the times in its price table when only the remaining part of the day is available. Earlier hours with no supplied prices stay blank instead of stretching the remaining periods across the whole day.

Update through HACS and restart Home Assistant, then reload the dashboard. This corrects the display; it does not change charging schedules or tariff prices.
