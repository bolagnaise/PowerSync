## What's Changed

**Shorter AI plan explanations**
Explanations now focus on the current plan, the next change, the reason and estimated cost, with the same length limits for every AI provider. The timeline and forecast inputs sit under **Details**, with five-minute readings behind a separate disclosure. Higher estimated costs are described clearly instead of as negative savings; refresh your explanation after updating for the new wording.

**Clearer reserve protection and export decisions**
Planned Battery Windows now explains when reserve protection holds or shortens a current export request, while **Reserve details** separates your minimum, the forecast recommendation, the active software floor and any trusted saved hardware reserve. Notices expire with their matching plan and time slot, and distinguish an accepted command from verified battery activity.
