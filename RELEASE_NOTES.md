## What's Changed

### GoodWe startup plans appear sooner
If GoodWe readings are still loading at startup, PowerSync now starts the first optimizer plan when they recover and startup battery restoration finishes, without waiting for the next five-minute interval. Update through HACS and restart Home Assistant; Monitoring Mode and manual battery controls still take precedence.

Update available via HACS.
